Grey Oyster / Elm Oyster

Oyster Mushroom Miso Ramen

Japanese

Time
40 min
Difficulty
Medium
Serves
2
Cuisine
Japanese / Korean

Grey oysters carry deep umami that builds the broth from the inside. A tare of white miso and soy, soft-boiled egg, nori, spring onion. A bowl that earns its warmth.

Ingredients
  • 200 g Grey Oyster mushrooms, clusters kept whole
  • 2 portions ramen noodles (or thin wheat noodles)
  • 1 litre water or light vegetable stock
  • 2 tbsp white miso paste
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp mirin (or 1/2 tsp sugar)
  • 3 cloves garlic, smashed
  • 1 inch ginger, sliced
  • 1 piece kombu (dried kelp), 10 cm — optional but highly recommended
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 sheets nori (dried seaweed), halved
  • 2 spring onions, thinly sliced
  • Sesame seeds for garnish
  • Chilli oil for serving
Method
  1. 01Soft-boil eggs: bring water to a boil, gently lower eggs in, cook exactly 6 minutes 30 seconds. Transfer immediately to an ice bath. Peel and marinate in a mix of 3 tbsp soy sauce + 1 tbsp mirin + 3 tbsp water for at least 30 minutes. These are ajitsuke tamago — marinated ramen eggs.
  2. 02Build the broth: in a pot, combine water or stock with garlic, ginger, and kombu if using. Bring to a near simmer (do not boil kombu — remove it when the water reaches 60°C). Simmer for 20 minutes.
  3. 03Add the grey oyster clusters whole to the broth. Simmer for 8–10 minutes. They will absorb the broth deeply and release their umami in return.
  4. 04Remove from heat. Whisk miso paste into the hot (not boiling) broth — boiling destroys the miso's live cultures and flavour. Add soy sauce, sesame oil, and mirin. Taste and adjust.
  5. 05Cook noodles separately according to package instructions. Drain well.
  6. 06Assemble: noodles in the bowl, ladle hot broth and mushrooms over, halve the marinated egg and place alongside, add nori, spring onion, sesame seeds, and a drizzle of chilli oil. Eat immediately.
Cook's Note
Grey oyster mushroom's umami depth is closest to shiitake — which is why it works so naturally in Japanese broth cooking. The kombu is worth finding; it brings a mineral quality that makes the broth taste like it has been cooking for hours. You can find it at specialty grocery stores in Nungambakkam and Alwarpet in Chennai, or online. The marinated egg is not optional — it is half the bowl.
