Golden Oyster / Grey Oyster

Oyster Mushroom Onigiri

Japanese Rice Balls

Time
25 min
Difficulty
Easy
Serves
4
Cuisine
Japanese / Korean

Soy-glazed golden oyster mushrooms folded into seasoned Japanese rice, shaped into triangles, wrapped in nori. A portable, perfectly-balanced lunch that's become a fixture at Chennai's artisan food markets.

Ingredients
  • 150 g Golden or Grey Oyster mushrooms, finely chopped
  • 2 cups Japanese short-grain rice (or sushi rice)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp mirin
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
  • 4–6 sheets nori, cut into strips or full sheets
  • Salt and water for shaping
Method
  1. 01Cook the rice with slightly less water than usual (the rice should be sticky and firm, not soft). While still warm, season with a pinch of salt and a few drops of sesame oil. Mix gently and cover to keep warm.
  2. 02Heat butter in a pan over medium-high heat. Add garlic and fry for 30 seconds. Add the finely chopped mushrooms — they should sizzle immediately. Cook for 4–5 minutes until all moisture evaporates and the mushrooms are dry and beginning to caramelise.
  3. 03Add soy sauce and mirin. Stir on high heat for 1–2 minutes until the sauce absorbs fully and the mushrooms are glazed and slightly sticky. Add sesame seeds. Cool the filling slightly.
  4. 04Wet your hands lightly with water and rub a small amount of salt between your palms. Take a tennis-ball-sized portion of warm rice. Make an indent, place a spoonful of mushroom filling in the centre, and mold the rice into a triangle around it, pressing firmly.
  5. 05Wrap the base with a nori strip, or encase the full bottom in a nori sheet. The nori will soften slightly and stick.
  6. 06Eat immediately while warm, or wrap tightly in plastic and refrigerate for up to 6 hours. Do not refrigerate uncovered — the rice will harden.
Cook's Note
The soy-butter glaze on golden oyster mushrooms is one of the most satisfying things you can put inside rice. The mushrooms must be completely dry before the soy goes in — if they are still wet, the filling will make the rice soggy. Japanese short-grain rice is available at most good supermarkets in Chennai. The shaping takes a little practice; the first one is always imperfect. That is fine — eat it.
