Elm Oyster / Grey Oyster

Mushroom Manchurian

Indo-Chinese Classic

Time
30 min
Difficulty
Medium
Serves
3
Cuisine
Indo-Chinese

Large torn clusters battered in seasoned corn flour, fried shatter-crisp, then tossed in a soy-ginger-garlic manchurian sauce that's sticky, sharp, and savoury. The mushroom's natural umami makes meat unnecessary.

Ingredients
  • 300 g Elm or Grey Oyster mushrooms, torn into large clusters
  • 4 tbsp corn flour
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp soy sauce (for batter)
  • Oil for deep frying
  • SAUCE
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp garlic, finely minced
  • 1 tsp ginger, finely minced
  • 3 green chillies, slit
  • 1/2 cup spring onion, white part chopped; greens for garnish
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp red chilli sauce
  • 1 tbsp vinegar (rice or white)
  • 1 tsp corn flour dissolved in 3 tbsp water
  • Salt & pepper to taste
Method
  1. 01Make the batter: mix corn flour, maida, pepper, and soy sauce with enough water to form a thick coating batter — it should coat the back of a spoon. Season with a little salt.
  2. 02Dip mushroom clusters in the batter, letting excess drip off. Deep-fry in hot oil (175°C) in batches for 3–4 minutes until golden and very crisp. Drain on a rack, not paper — the rack keeps the crust crispier.
  3. 03For the sauce: heat 2 tbsp oil in a wok on high heat. Add garlic, ginger, and green chillies — stir for 30 seconds until fragrant but not brown.
  4. 04Add the white part of spring onion and toss for 1 minute. Add soy sauce, chilli sauce, and vinegar. Let it bubble for 30 seconds.
  5. 05Add the corn flour slurry and stir as the sauce thickens, about 45 seconds. It should be glossy and coat the back of a spoon.
  6. 06Add the fried mushrooms and toss hard to coat. Serve immediately garnished with spring onion greens. It goes soggy within minutes — eat while the crust still has crunch.
Cook's Note
The key is to fry the mushrooms twice — once to set the crust, and once more at higher heat just before saucing to get them shatter-crisp. Keep the sauce on one side of the wok while you add the mushrooms on the other, then combine quickly. Oyster mushroom already has umami — you do not need MSG. The soy sauce handles it.
