Ingredients
  • 150 g Pink Oyster mushrooms, torn into thin strips
  • 200 g Hakka noodles (or egg noodles / thin wheat noodles)
  • 3 tbsp oil (peanut or sesame)
  • 1 tbsp garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp ginger, minced
  • 1/2 cup carrot, julienned
  • 1/2 cup cabbage, shredded
  • 1/2 cup capsicum, julienned
  • 3 spring onions, white and green separated
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp red chilli sauce
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp sesame oil (for finish)
  • Salt to taste
Method
  1. 01Boil noodles in well-salted water until just cooked — do not overcook. Drain, toss immediately with a few drops of oil to prevent sticking. Set aside on a wide plate to cool and dry slightly.
  2. 02Heat wok on the highest heat your stove allows until it begins to smoke. Add oil, then garlic and ginger — 20 seconds, not more.
  3. 03Add the pink oyster mushrooms and let them sit without stirring for 60 seconds so they begin to sear. Then toss. The pink colour will deepen to coral-red at the edges.
  4. 04Add carrot, capsicum, and white spring onion. Toss on high heat for 2 minutes — the vegetables should retain their crunch.
  5. 05Add cabbage, soy sauce, chilli sauce, vinegar, and pepper. Toss for 1 minute.
  6. 06Add the cooked noodles and toss everything together over high heat for 2 minutes, using tongs to separate the noodle strands. Finish with sesame oil and spring onion greens. Serve immediately.
Cook's Note
Pink oyster tears along the grain into long, thin shreds — which is exactly the texture you want in noodles. Do not pre-cut them; use your hands. Wok hei (the smoky char from high heat) is everything in this dish. Cook in a smaller batch if your stove cannot handle a full portion at once — the wok temperature drop is the enemy.
