Grey Oyster / Elm Oyster

Kadai Kalan

Kadai Mushroom

Time
30 min
Difficulty
Easy
Serves
3
Cuisine
North Indian

Freshly ground kadai masala — coriander, dried chilli, cloves — hits a screaming wok with onion and capsicum before torn oyster clusters get tossed in. The charred edges and silky mushroom texture are worth the smoke.

Ingredients
  • 350 g Grey or Elm Oyster mushrooms, torn into large pieces
  • 1 large capsicum, cut in strips
  • 2 medium onions — 1 roughly chopped, 1 sliced in petals
  • 3 tomatoes, roughly chopped
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 2 tsp coriander seeds, crushed coarsely
  • 4–5 dried red chillies, broken
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 inch ginger, grated
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander leaves, chopped
  • Salt to taste
Method
  1. 01Dry-roast the coriander seeds and dried red chillies in a heavy pan until fragrant. Cool and grind coarsely. This is your fresh kadai masala — it makes all the difference.
  2. 02Heat oil in a kadai or wide wok on high heat. Add cumin seeds and let them sizzle. Add the chopped onion and fry until golden, about 6 minutes.
  3. 03Add garlic and ginger, fry for 1 minute. Add tomatoes, turmeric, and chilli powder. Cook on medium-high until the tomatoes are fully broken down and the oil separates, about 8 minutes.
  4. 04Add the mushrooms, capsicum strips, onion petals, and the ground kadai masala. Toss on high heat for 4–5 minutes — the goal is charred edges on the vegetables and mushrooms while keeping some bite.
  5. 05Finish with garam masala, salt, and fresh coriander. Serve immediately with roti or naan. The heat must stay high — this dish is defined by its sear.
Cook's Note
Kadai mushroom lives and dies on heat. You need the highest flame your stove can produce and a wide pan with enough surface area that the mushrooms sear rather than steam. If the pan is crowded, they will release water and the dish will become a stew. Fry in two batches if needed, then combine at the end with the masala.
