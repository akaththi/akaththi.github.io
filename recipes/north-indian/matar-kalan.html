Ingredients
  • 300 g Elm Oyster mushrooms, clusters halved
  • 1 cup green peas, fresh or frozen
  • 2 large onions, finely chopped
  • 3 tomatoes, pureed
  • 12 cashews, soaked 15 min
  • 3 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 4 cloves garlic, paste
  • 1 inch ginger, paste
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp fresh cream (optional)
  • Salt to taste
Method
  1. 01Blend soaked cashews with a few tablespoons of water into a smooth, thick paste. Set aside.
  2. 02Heat oil in a heavy pan. Add cumin seeds and bay leaf. Add onions and fry on medium heat until deeply golden, about 12 minutes.
  3. 03Add ginger-garlic paste and fry for 2 minutes. Add tomato puree, turmeric, chilli powder, and coriander powder. Cook until the masala dries out and oil separates, about 10 minutes.
  4. 04Add the cashew paste and stir for 2 minutes. Add 200 ml water and bring to a simmer.
  5. 05Add mushrooms and peas. Simmer on medium heat for 10–12 minutes until the gravy thickens and clings to the mushrooms.
  6. 06Stir in garam masala and cream if using. Taste and adjust salt. Serve with butter naan, paratha, or steamed basmati rice.
Cook's Note
The cashew paste is what gives this dish a restaurant-grade richness without dairy overload. Elm oyster holds its shape beautifully in a simmered gravy — the clusters stay intact and absorb the masala from the outside in. If using frozen peas, add them in the last 5 minutes so they stay bright and sweet.
