Elm Oyster / Grey Oyster

Oyster Mushroom Kati Roll

Street-style Wrap

Time
20 min
Difficulty
Easy
Serves
2
Cuisine
Rolls & Grills

Spiced oyster mushrooms pan-roasted with onion and green chilli, rolled in a soft paratha with raw onion, chaat masala, and green chutney. Street food that eats like a meal.

Ingredients
  • 250 g Elm or Grey Oyster mushrooms, torn into thick strips
  • 4 soft parathas or roti (store-bought is fine)
  • 1 large onion — half sliced thin, half diced raw
  • 1 small capsicum, sliced
  • 2 green chillies, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp chaat masala
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • Green chutney coriander-mint
  • Lemon juice to taste
  • Salt to taste
Method
  1. 01Heat oil in a wide pan over high heat. Add cumin seeds and let them sizzle. Add sliced onion and capsicum — cook on high heat for 4 minutes until the onion is translucent and beginning to char at the edges.
  2. 02Add green chillies and mushrooms. Spread them out and let them sear for 2 minutes before stirring. Season with turmeric, chilli powder, and salt. Toss on high heat for another 3–4 minutes until the mushrooms are cooked through with charred edges.
  3. 03Finish with garam masala and chaat masala. Taste — it should be bold, spiced, and slightly smoky.
  4. 04Warm the parathas one at a time in the same pan over medium heat, pressing flat. A little oil helps.
  5. 05To assemble: lay paratha flat, spread green chutney across the centre, pile the mushroom filling in a line across the middle. Add raw diced onion, a squeeze of lemon, and a pinch of chaat masala.
  6. 06Roll tightly, wrap the bottom half in foil or paper to hold. Serve immediately.
Cook's Note
The raw diced onion at the end is non-negotiable — it provides crunch and freshness against the hot, spiced mushrooms. The chaat masala is also what separates a kati roll from a basic wrap. Elm oyster mushrooms hold their texture even when tossed hard over high heat, which makes them ideal for this treatment. Do not use pink oyster here — it breaks down too fast.
