Elm Oyster / Pink Oyster

BBQ Oyster Mushroom Skewers

Grilled with Smoky Glaze

Time
25 min
Difficulty
Easy
Serves
4
Cuisine
Rolls & Grills

Whole oyster clusters threaded on skewers, brushed with a tamarind-chilli-soy glaze, and grilled over high heat until the edges char and the glaze lacquers. A natural fit for terrace evenings and barbecue spreads.

Ingredients
  • 350 g Elm or Pink Oyster mushrooms, kept in whole clusters
  • 8–10 wooden or metal skewers
  • GLAZE
  • 3 tbsp tamarind paste (thick)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey or jaggery, dissolved
  • 1 tbsp red chilli sauce or sriracha
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika or chilli powder
  • 2 cloves garlic, grated
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • TO SERVE
  • Lemon wedges
  • Chaat masala or smoked salt
  • Fresh coriander
Method
  1. 01If using wooden skewers, soak in cold water for 30 minutes to prevent burning.
  2. 02Whisk together all glaze ingredients. Taste — it should be sticky, sour, smoky, and hot. Adjust chilli or tamarind to your preference.
  3. 03Thread whole mushroom clusters onto skewers, piercing through the base of each cluster so the fronds fan outward. Do not overcrowd — leave space for the heat to reach all surfaces.
  4. 04Brush generously with the glaze on all sides. Reserve remaining glaze for basting.
  5. 05Grill over high direct heat — barbecue, cast-iron griddle pan, or under a high oven grill. Cook 3–4 minutes per side, basting once more mid-cook. The glaze should blacken at the very tips and edges — that char is not a mistake, it's the point.
  6. 06Remove from heat. Brush with one final coat of glaze while hot. Rest 2 minutes, then dust with chaat masala, scatter coriander, and serve with lemon wedges.
Cook's Note
Tamarind and soy is a pairing that feels obvious in retrospect — the tartness of tamarind echoes the rice-vinegar notes in soy, and together they caramelise faster than either alone. Pink oyster mushrooms go spectacular on the grill — the frilled edges char and crisp while the base stays meaty. Elm oyster holds together better on the skewer if you need a more stable presentation.
