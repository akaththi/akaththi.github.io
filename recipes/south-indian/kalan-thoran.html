Pink Oyster / Golden Oyster

Kalan Thoran

Mushroom Coconut Stir-Fry

Time
15 min
Difficulty
Easy
Serves
3
Cuisine
South Indian

Fresh coconut, mustard seeds, green chilli, and turmeric — all in one pan, done in minutes. Pink oyster turns this humble thoran into something you'd find at a farm-table feast.

Ingredients
  • 250 g Pink or Golden Oyster mushrooms, torn or roughly chopped
  • 3/4 cup fresh coconut, grated
  • 2 green chillies, slit
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil
  • 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 sprig curry leaves
  • 2 dried red chillies, broken
  • Salt to taste
Method
  1. 01Coarsely grind the grated coconut with green chillies, turmeric, and cumin seeds — just a few pulses, keeping it textured, not smooth.
  2. 02In a wide pan, heat coconut oil over medium heat. Add mustard seeds and wait for them to pop. Add dried red chillies and curry leaves, stir for 20 seconds.
  3. 03Add the torn mushrooms with a pinch of salt. Stir-fry on medium-high heat for 3–4 minutes until they release their moisture and begin to colour slightly.
  4. 04Add the coconut mixture. Stir well to coat, then reduce heat to medium-low. Cook for another 3–4 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the coconut is heated through and fragrant.
  5. 05Taste and adjust salt. Serve as a side dish with steamed rice, sambar, and rasam.
Cook's Note
Pink oyster mushrooms are visually spectacular in this dish — they hold their coral colour against the white coconut and green chilli. Golden oyster brings a slightly sweeter, nuttier character. Either works. Use freshly grated coconut if you can; frozen is acceptable, but the dry coconut sold in packets will make this dish flat.
