Good Food.

Flavour comes first. Every variety we grow — Elm, Pink, Golden, Grey Oyster and more — was chosen because it deserves a place on a serious plate. Not for novelty. Not for photographs. Because it tastes extraordinary, and you will know the difference.

02
Good Science.

Precision is an act of care. Our proprietary POD micro-farm chambers and KAANI Intelligence hold every variable — temperature, humidity, CO₂ — so the mushroom can grow exactly as nature designed it, without compromise. Consistent quality isn't luck. It's attention.

03
Honestly Grown.

No pesticides. No imported substitutes. No cold chain delays. Every batch grown on substrate made from agricultural crop waste — rice straw, farm residue — material that would otherwise be burned into the sky. What was waste becomes food. That is what honest growing looks like.

Our Vision
01

Food closer
to its roots.
That's it.

Food is more than something you eat. It is a memory. A relationship. A thread back to the earth and the people who tend it. In a world that has drifted far from its food, we exist to close that distance — one harvest at a time.

Our mushrooms grow from what the harvest leaves behind. Crop residue. Agricultural by-products. Material the system had no use for. We transform it into something nourishing and extraordinary. Not because it is clever. Because it is right.

We are Wild at Heart. We grow the way nature intended — with honesty, science, and a stubborn refusal to take shortcuts.

"The farmer does not fight nature. The farmer listens to it."

Our Commitment
02

Grown from
waste. Built
for zero.

India burns millions of tonnes of crop residue every year. The smoke turns our skies orange every winter. For most, it is an inconvenience. For us, it was a question — what if this became food instead?

Every batch of Akaththí mushrooms grows on substrate made from agricultural waste: rice straw, farm by-products, material the field no longer needs. We take what would have been ash and turn it into something alive. The mushrooms feed on it. We harvest. The spent substrate goes back to enrich the soil. Nothing is lost.

We are on the road to 100% sustainability. Net zero agriculture is not a headline we put on packaging — it is the direction every decision here points. Circular inputs. Minimal energy. Zero chemical load. A farm that takes less than it gives.
