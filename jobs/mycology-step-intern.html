STEP Internship Program

Mycology

Work with real fungi, real cultures, and real science — not textbook diagrams.

Location Chennai (Onsite)
Schedule 9 AM – 5 PM, Onsite
Duration 1 Month
Intake 2 students / month
Compensation Unpaid

What you'll
work on

  • Spawn preparation and inoculation — liquid culture, grain spawn, sawdust blocks
  • Substrate formulation and pasteurisation across species
  • Fruiting chamber management: CO₂, humidity, temperature, airflow
  • Contamination identification, isolation, and control protocols
  • Culture work: agar plates, tissue culture, strain observation
  • Harvest timing, post-harvest handling, and basic yield tracking

What you'll
walk away with

  • STEP Internship Certificate
  • Hands-on Mushroom Cultivation Certificate
  • Tissue Culture & Spawn Production Certificate
  • End-to-end understanding of commercial oyster mushroom cultivation
  • Real experience that builds confidence — not just a line in your resume

Who can apply

Students and recent graduates in Microbiology, Biotechnology, Botany, Food Technology, or a related field. You don't need prior mushroom experience — but you need to be genuinely curious and not afraid of early mornings, substrate dust, and getting things wrong before getting them right.

  • B.Sc / M.Sc / B.Tech students or graduates in life sciences
  • Self-directed — able to observe, document, and ask good questions
  • Based in or able to commute to Chennai for the duration
  • Available for onsite work, 9 AM – 5 PM

BYOD — Bring Your Own Device

You are expected to bring your own laptop or tablet for documentation and data recording. We provide lab access, materials, and equipment — not workstations.
