STEP Internship Program

Automation

The grow room runs on precision. Come build the systems behind it.

Location Chennai (Onsite)
Schedule 9 AM – 5 PM, Onsite
Duration 1 Month
Intake Rolling
Compensation Unpaid

What you'll
work on

  • Build and maintain climate control automation for fruiting chambers — humidity, CO₂, temperature, airflow
  • Write scripts and automation for environment scheduling and alert systems
  • Integrate sensor data into monitoring dashboards
  • Identify manual workflows that can be automated and prototype the solutions
  • Document automation logic so it can be reproduced and handed off
  • Work directly with the grow team to understand the biological requirements driving each system

What you'll
walk away with

  • STEP Internship Certificate
  • Hands-on experience building automation systems in a live production environment
  • Understanding of how precision agriculture controls work end-to-end
  • Documented systems you actually built — not simulations
  • A clear sense of what automation means when biology is in the loop

Who can apply

Students in ECE, EEE, CS, IT, or any engineering discipline with a genuine interest in automation and control systems. You don't need prior farm experience — but you need to be comfortable working in a hands-on, live environment where the systems you build affect a real operation.

  • Java — working knowledge required for automation scripting and backend tooling
  • Docker & Kubernetes — ability to containerise and deploy automation services
  • Comfortable with Python, Node-RED, or any additional scripting tooling
  • Self-directed — can scope a problem, attempt a solution, and document what you tried
  • Based in Chennai for the duration of the internship

BYOD — Bring Your Own Device

You are expected to bring your own laptop. We provide farm access, hardware, and tooling — not workstations.
