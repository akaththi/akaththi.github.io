STEP Internship Program

Sales

We're selling something most of Chennai hasn't tasted yet. That's a hard, interesting problem.

LocationChennai (Hybrid)
Schedule9 AM – 5 PM
Duration1 Month
IntakeRolling
CompensationUnpaid

What you'll
work on

  • Identify and approach restaurants, cafes, and hotels for B2B standing orders
  • Manage a D2C pipeline via WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, and social channels
  • Visit potential clients — kitchens, specialty stores, and pop-up markets
  • Track outreach and document what converts and what doesn't
  • Understand buyer objections and help refine the pitch
  • Coordinate sample deliveries and follow up on trial orders

What you'll
walk away with

  • STEP Internship Certificate
  • Real B2B sales experience in food — actual accounts, actual conversations
  • Understanding of how a producer sells to restaurants and retailers
  • A clear picture of what it takes to build a sales pipeline from scratch
  • Thick skin and the confidence to walk into a kitchen cold

Who can apply

Any student with strong communication skills and a genuine interest in how businesses sell things. No prior sales experience required — but you need to be comfortable talking to strangers, comfortable with rejection, and ready to be on the road.

  • Confident in conversation — in person, on the phone, and in writing
  • Interested in food, hospitality, or how consumer businesses grow
  • Self-directed — can manage a pipeline and follow up without being told
  • Two-wheeler required — field visits to restaurants, cafes, and stores are a core part of this role. You need your own vehicle and a valid licence
  • Based in Chennai

BYOD — Bring Your Own Device

You are expected to bring your own phone and laptop for client communication, CRM tracking, and outreach. We provide tools, product samples, and access — not hardware.

How to Apply

Email us.

job@akaththi.in

Subject line Application for Sales STEP Intern – Your Name

Attach your resume and a cover letter. Tell us about something you've sold — a product, an idea, a project you pitched. What was hard about it and what did you learn?

We run STEP internships year-round. We're open to curious students and open-source contributors — not certificate collectors.
