STEP Internship Program

Supply
Chain

Fresh produce is unforgiving. We need people who obsess over the last mile.

LocationChennai (Onsite)
Schedule9 AM – 5 PM, Onsite
Duration1 Month
IntakeRolling
CompensationUnpaid

What you'll
work on

  • Map and optimise delivery routes for B2B and D2C orders across Chennai
  • Manage procurement: substrate materials, packaging, lab and farm supplies
  • Maintain inventory tracking and coordinate with the harvest team on demand planning
  • Identify cold-chain gaps and propose practical, low-cost fixes
  • Support vendor relationships and help negotiate rates on recurring purchases
  • Document processes so they can be handed off as we grow

What you'll
walk away with

  • STEP Internship Certificate
  • End-to-end supply chain experience for fresh perishable produce
  • Understanding of cold-chain logistics and last-mile delivery constraints
  • Real operations exposure from procurement to customer delivery
  • Direct involvement in decisions that affect product quality on the shelf

Who can apply

Students in Supply Chain Management, Operations, Logistics, BBA, or MBA. You need to be comfortable in the field, not just behind a spreadsheet — this role involves farm visits, vendor meetings, and delivery coordination. A real operations mindset matters more than a specific degree.

  • Interest in operations, logistics, or how physical goods move from source to customer
  • Comfortable with planning tools, spreadsheets, and basic data tracking
  • Willing to be on-site and in the field, not just remote
  • Based in Chennai for the duration of the internship

BYOD — Bring Your Own Device

You are expected to bring your own laptop for planning, tracking, and documentation work. Farm access, vendor contacts, and any field tools needed for operations will be provided.
