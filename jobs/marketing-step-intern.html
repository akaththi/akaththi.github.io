Marketing

LocationChennai (Hybrid)

What you'll
work on

  • Plan and execute digital marketing campaigns for D2C and B2B audiences in Chennai
  • Write and schedule content across Instagram, WhatsApp Broadcast, and email
  • Research keywords and develop SEO content for the website
  • Track campaign performance and surface weekly insights
  • Set up and manage basic email marketing flows
  • Identify new channels and audiences worth testing

What you'll
walk away with

  • STEP Internship Certificate
  • A real campaign portfolio — not mock projects or college assignments
  • Understanding of how a food brand grows digitally in an emerging market
  • Exposure to both D2C and B2B marketing strategies in the same role
  • Working knowledge of analytics, content planning, and channel strategy

Who can apply

Students in Marketing, Mass Communication, BBA, or anyone who has taught themselves digital marketing. You need to understand both language and numbers — a good campaign has a clear story and measurable results, and you should care about both.

  • Comfortable with basic analytics (Google Analytics, Instagram Insights, or similar)
  • Can write clearly and concisely — good copy is foundational here
  • Basic understanding of SEO, social reach, and paid vs organic channels
  • Two-wheeler required — field content shoots at the farm, market visits, and pop-up events are part of this role. You need your own vehicle and a valid licence
  • Based in Chennai

BYOD — Bring Your Own Device

You are expected to bring your own phone and laptop. A phone with a decent camera is enough for content shoots. We provide access, creative direction, and brand assets — not hardware.
