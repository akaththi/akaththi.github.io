Our Story

Ammavin
Malaikaalan
Kulambu.

My love for mushrooms began in childhood. After every rainfall, my Amma would forage wild mushrooms from the fields and turn them into her special dish — Malaikaalan Kulambu. The taste of that curry, earthy and full of life, stayed with me forever.

"Those flavours were more than food. They were memories of nature, of family, of something pure."

Years later, when curiosity turned into a deeper passion, I knew I wanted to bring that same freshness and magic of mushrooms into people's lives.

That passion became the seed of Akaththi Farms. Built on curiosity, care, and respect for nature, it is where tradition meets science. We recreate the natural environments mushrooms love — using sustainable micro-farm systems to cultivate exotic varieties like Pink Oyster, Golden Oyster, Elm Oyster, Lion's Mane, Shimeji, Enoki, Shiitake, Pioppino, and many more.

Akaththi Farms was born not from business plans, but from a love for mushrooms — their flavour, their beauty, and their promise for the future of food. Every harvest we bring to your table carries that same spirit. Fresh and Wild at Heart.

— Thirumalaivasan Rajasekaran, Founder

Our spawn sourcing and substrate formulation protocols are developed in collaboration with IIHR (Indian Institute of Horticultural Research), ensuring scientific rigour at the foundation.
Tamil Nadu Roots
Salem-registered, Polichalur-based. Our supply chain is hyperlocal — we grow for Chennai kitchens, deliver same-day, and trace every mushroom back to its batch.
Built to go Global
Our model is designed to scale beyond India. POD infrastructure is leased — not franchised. Sales expansion is in pipeline, bringing the same quality and traceability to urban centres worldwide.
