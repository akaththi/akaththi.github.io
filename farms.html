Process

How we grow
our mushrooms.

Three steps. Nothing skipped. Nothing outsourced.

01
Strain Culture
Spawn it.

Our cultures are developed in-house — strain-specific, formulated for our substrate and climate. It's where quality starts. Long before a spore enters a POD, the work has already begun.

02
POD
Grow it.

Each POD holds its own climate — temperature, humidity, CO₂ — set for the variety inside. Batches don't share space. What happens in one doesn't affect another.

03
KAANI Intelligence
Track it.

Sensor data runs continuously through KAANI. Growth patterns, deviations, harvest readiness — all of it visible, all of it logged. The mushroom tells you when it's ready. We just listen.

Start to finish, every batch has a record. That's what makes it repeatable — and what makes it worth trusting.

Technology

KAANI
Intelligence.

Vertical farming has a cost problem. Infrastructure that scales fast. Operations that compound faster. KAANI was built to change that equation — an indigenous farm operating system that runs lean, thinks locally, and grows with you.

Node. Edge. Cloud. Each layer designed to operate alone if it has to. Power drops. Connections break. KAANI was designed for that reality — the edge holds when the cloud can't reach, the node holds when everything else goes down. The crop never feels it.

And because we control every condition, we can grow what the land here wouldn't otherwise allow. Varieties that need cold. Varieties that need precision. Grown sustainably, year after year — not despite our climate, but independent of it.

Node · Edge · Cloud

Intelligence lives close to life. Nodes sense inside every POD. Edge processors think on-site, without waiting. The cloud learns across every farm, every season. Three layers that trust each other — so you can trust what comes out.

Indigenous Hybrid Cooling

Our summers are unforgiving. We built our cooling system knowing that — a hybrid approach, engineered in-house, that keeps each POD in its own precise world. The mushroom doesn't know it's forty degrees outside. That's the point.

Indigenous Micro-Farm Architecture

Nothing in a POD was borrowed. Every dimension, every airflow path, every substrate surface was designed around how mushrooms actually grow. It shows in the consistency. It shows in the flavour.

AI-Embedded Architecture

The intelligence doesn't sit at a distance making remote decisions. It runs at every layer — learning from each batch, reading readiness before a human would notice, flagging what doesn't look right before it becomes a problem.

Distributed Farm Operations

KAANI was built to outlast a single farm. Bring a new location online — the system already speaks the language. One operating layer, many farms, anywhere. The vision was always bigger than Polichalur.

Our Farm

Polichalur
Farm.

Cultivation & Research Hub

Polichalur is where we start. Our mycology lab, our PODs, our substrate formulation — all under one roof in Tamil Nadu. This is where KAANI Intelligence runs 24/7, where strain cultures are developed, and where every batch begins its record.

Akaththí Farms Private Limited is registered in Salem, Tamil Nadu. But the thinking here was never local. Every system we've built — the architecture, the intelligence, the farm design — was made to travel. Polichalur is farm one.
