01
Good Food.

Flavour comes first. Every variety we grow — Elm, Pink, Golden Oyster and more — was chosen because it deserves a place on a serious plate. Not for novelty. Not for photographs. Because it tastes extraordinary, and you will know the difference.

02
Good Science.

Precision is an act of care. Our proprietary POD micro-farm chambers and KAANI Intelligence hold every variable — temperature, humidity, CO₂ — so the mushroom can grow exactly as nature designed it, without compromise. Consistent quality isn't luck. It's attention.

03
Honestly Grown.

No pesticides. No imported substitutes. No cold chain delays. Every batch grown on substrate made from agricultural crop waste — rice straw, farm residue — material that would otherwise be burned into the sky. What was waste becomes food. That is what honest growing looks like.

Our Vision
01

Food closer
to its roots.
That's it.

Food is more than something you eat. It is a memory. A relationship. A thread back to the earth and the people who tend it. In a world that has drifted far from its food, we exist to close that distance — one harvest at a time.

Our mushrooms grow from what the harvest leaves behind. Crop residue. Agricultural by-products. Material the system had no use for. We transform it into something nourishing and extraordinary. Not because it is clever. Because it is right.

We are Wild at Heart. We grow the way nature intended — with honesty, science, and a stubborn refusal to take shortcuts.

Process

How we grow.

Three steps. Nothing skipped. Nothing outsourced.

01
Strain Culture
Spawn it.

Our cultures are developed in-house — strain-specific, formulated for our substrate and climate. It's where quality starts. Long before a spore enters a POD, the work has already begun.

02
POD
Grow it.

Each POD holds its own climate — temperature, humidity, CO₂ — set for the variety inside. Batches don't share space. What happens in one doesn't affect another.

03
KAANI Intelligence
Track it.

Sensor data runs continuously through KAANI. Growth patterns, deviations, harvest readiness — all of it visible, all of it logged. The mushroom tells you when it's ready. We just listen.

Start to finish, every batch has a record. That's what makes it repeatable — and what makes it worth trusting.
